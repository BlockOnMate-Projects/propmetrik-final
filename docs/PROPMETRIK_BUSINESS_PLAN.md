## 1. Executive Summary

**What we are.** PROPMETRIK is a real-estate intelligence and operations platform for Ghana, built to become the **operating system for real estate in West Africa** — and, over time, the **data and capital-markets infrastructure** that makes African real estate investable.

**What we've built.** A fully-developed, multi-product platform (≈513,000 lines of code) spanning **four revenue services** — Valuation, Property Management, Project Management, and Deal Management — plus a **Data Hub** (a complete data pipeline) and a **Market Intelligence / analytics** layer with four proprietary indices. It is built; it is **not yet launched**.

**How we make money.** Subscription SaaS across the four services (priced in GHS today), plus **data and analytics sold to B2B institutions and government agencies** (API licensing, market-intelligence subscriptions, and custom/volume licensing).

**The long game (Vision 2040).** Every service generates proprietary data; the Data Hub standardises it; the analytics layer monetises it. That same data foundation is the substrate for a phased ascent into **credit intelligence → servicing → mortgage/asset refinancing → tokenized issuance → a secondary market for African real-estate-backed securities.** The company's own stated vision — a market where every property has *"a known value, verified history, and a liquid future"* — already contains this endgame. (Detailed in the *Vision 2040* dossier.)

**The ask.** **US$450,000** to launch, land the first paying customers across the services, and begin filling the data pipeline — the milestones that de-risk a seed round. With a Ghana cost base, this funds a focused five-person team for **~22 months**.

**Why it's fundable now.** Product risk is largely retired (the platform is built, lean/solo); the capital required is small and efficient (Ghana costs); the data moat compounds from day one; and the near-term business stands on its own while the long-term vision offers venture-scale optionality.

## 3. Problem & Market Opportunity

**The problem.** African real-estate markets are **opaque and fragmented**: buyers distrust prices, lenders question collateral value, developers operate on assumptions, and institutional capital cannot reach the asset class. The root cause is **data** — most transactions lack verifiable, standardised information, and the data that does exist is *market intelligence* (prices, comps), **not** the transaction-level, performance-tracked, capital-markets-grade data that underwriting and securitisation require.

**Why now.**
- **Digital rails have matured:** national identity (Ghana Card/NIA), mobile money (MoMo/GhIPSS), and registry digitisation (Lands Commission).
- **Capital is opening up:** Ghana now mandates **5% of pension assets to VC/PE (~US$300M/yr)**; African fintech/proptech funding is at multi-year highs.
- **No incumbent owns the full stack** — operations + standardised data + capital-markets rails — for African real estate.

**Market sizing (framework — figures to source).**
- **TAM:** Ghana's real-estate sector *[to source: market value]*; broader West-/Pan-African real estate for the long-term vision.
- **SAM:** institutions and professionals who buy real-estate software and data — banks, valuation firms, developers, agencies, landlords, insurers, government.
- **SOM (near-term):** Ghanaian real-estate professionals and institutions reachable at launch.

## 4. Products & Services

PROPMETRIK is two engines that reinforce each other: **operational services that generate proprietary data**, and a **data/analytics layer that monetises it** — to B2B and government.

### 4.1 The four revenue services (operations + data acquisition)

| Service | What it does | Primary buyers | Indicative pricing (GHS/mo) | Data it generates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Valuation** | AI valuation engine (Sales Comparison, Cost, Income, Residual, Profits, DRC methods) with RICS-certified review; mortgage-ready reports; API | Banks, insurers, developers, valuation firms | 350 / 750 / 2,500 | values, comparables, cap rates |
| **Property Management** | Leases, tenants, maintenance, vendors, rent collection (Mobile Money), financial reporting | Landlords, property managers, institutions | 250 / 550 / 1,100 | rents, payments, occupancy |
| **Project Management** | Construction/development: budgets, draws, change orders, Gantt, procurement, contractors, compliance | Developers | 325 / 650 / 1,300 | construction cost, pipeline |
| **Deal Management (CRM)** | Pipelines, contacts, auto-generated agreements, e-signature, commissions, WhatsApp | Agents, brokers, developers | 199 / 499 / 999 | transactions, pricing |

*(E-signature is a feature enabling lease/agreement signing within these services — not a separate product.)*

### 4.2 The Data Hub (the pipeline / the moat)

A complete data-infrastructure layer: ingestion, ETL, contribution, **Ghana-Post geocoding, data catalog, data lineage, data-quality, anonymisation, scrapers, partner-API client, and ML integration.** It aggregates and standardises the four services' output into a single source of truth — and is the foundation for both the analytics product and the long-term capital-markets infrastructure. Exposed via a **developer API** (REST, SDKs).

### 4.3 Market Intelligence & indices (B2B + government)

Quarterly market reports (Residential, Commercial, Land), price and yield indices, and **four proprietary indices**:
- **GHPI** — Ghana House Price Index
- **GHAI** — Ghana Housing Affordability Index
- **CCI** — Construction Cost Index
- **GCPI** — Ghana Commercial Property Index

Delivered via dashboards, reports, Excel, and API. Sold to banks, funds, institutions, and — via **custom/volume licensing — government agencies and international entities.**

## 5. Business Model & Revenue Streams

**Revenue streams:**
1. **SaaS subscriptions** — the four services, tiered (individual → professional → enterprise), monthly/annual (annual discount).
2. **Data & API licensing** — Data Hub API access (tiered by rate/volume) and the indices.
3. **Market Intelligence subscriptions** — reports + dashboards (Analyst → Professional → Enterprise).
4. **Enterprise/government & volume licensing** — custom contracts for institutions, government agencies, and international entities.

**Characteristics:** recurring, software-margin revenue; capital-light at the company level; the data products carry the highest margin and strategic value (they are the moat monetising). À-la-carte module pricing lets customers adopt one service and expand.

**Revenue ramp (pre-launch reality):** zero at t=0; begins after launch as the first customers onboard across the four services, with data/analytics revenue following as the pipeline fills and the indices gain credibility. The detailed build is in the financial model.

## 7. Market, Competition & Expansion

**Competitive landscape — and why incumbents are partners, not competitors.** Existing African real-estate data players are **market-intelligence providers**, not operations-or-capital-markets infrastructure:
- **South Africa — Lightstone** (mature AVM/deeds data; SA also has developed REIT capital markets).
- **Nigeria — Estate Intel** (largest historic price/vacancy database; multi-country).
- **Kenya — HassConsult** (Hass Property Index), Cytonn; ArdhiSasa (registry).

None provide transaction-level performance data, a cross-border standard, or servicing/issuance rails. **PROPMETRIK operates the layer above them** — operations + performance data + capital-markets infrastructure. In data-rich markets we **partner/integrate** their data; in data-thin markets we **build** the pipeline.

**Expansion sequence.** Ghana (build, beachhead) → **Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya (2027 target)** → **South Africa later** (mature outlier; partner/benchmark). *Build where data is thin; partner where it exists.*

## 8. Go-to-Market Strategy

**Ideal customers (launch):** valuation firms and banks (Valuation + Data); developers (Project Management + Deal); agencies/brokers (Deal Management); landlords/PMs (Property Management); funds/government (Market Intelligence + Data).

**Motion:**
1. **Launch** the platform and convert design-partner/early interest into first paying subscriptions across the four services.
2. **Land-and-expand** via à-la-carte modules (adopt one service, add others).
3. **Seed the data flywheel** — every customer's activity fills the Data Hub, improving the indices and analytics, which in turn attract B2B/government data buyers.
4. **Sales hire** drives outbound to institutions; founder-led for the first lighthouse logos.

**The flywheel:** services → proprietary data → better analytics/indices → more credibility → more services + data buyers → more data. This compounding is the core GTM thesis.
