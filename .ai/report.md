The property is located within the Sekondi-Takoradi Metropolitan Area. Sekondi-Takoradi is a twin city located about 227 km west of Accra. It is the administrative capital of the Western Region. ## PART FOUR: VALUATION PROCESS

### BASIS OF VALUATION

The principle of Market Value forms the basis of this valuation.

### DEFINITION OF MARKET VALUE

For the purpose of this valuation "Market Value" as defined by the RICS Appraisal and Valuation Manual (1996) as the estimated amount for which an asset should exchange on the date of valuation between a willing seller and a buyer in an arms' length transaction after proper marketing, wherein the parties had each acted knowledgeably, prudently and without compulsion.

By the above definition:

a. **"The estimated amount"** means the most probable price expressed in terms of money to be reasonably obtained in the market at the date of valuation

b. **"a property should exchange"** means the fact that the value of a property is an estimated amount, rather than actual sale price

c. **"on the date of valuation"** means the estimated Market Value is time specific as of a given date. This is because markets and market conditions may change; the estimated value may be incorrect or inappropriate at another time

d. **"between a willing buyer"** means one who purchases in accordance with the realities of the current market. The assumed buyer would not pay a higher price than the market requires

e. **"a willing seller"** means a hypothetical owner who is motivated to sell the property at the best price attainable

f. **"in an arm's length transaction"** means a transaction which is between parties who do not have a particular or special relationship. It is therefore presumed to be between unrelated parties each acting independently

g. **"after proper marketing"** means the property would be exposed to the market in the most appropriate manner. The length of exposure time may vary with market conditions, but must be sufficient to allow adequate participation of potential purchasers

h. **"wherein the parties had each acted knowledgeably"** presumes the willing buyer and the willing seller are reasonably well informed about the nature and characteristics of the property

i. **"and without compulsion"** means that each party is motivated to undertake the transaction, but neither is forced or unduly coerced to complete it

### VALUATION METHODOLOGY

The Market Value of the property may be established by reference to any of the conventional methods of valuation, namely the Direct Comparison/Market Comparison, Income Capitalization and Depreciated Replacement Cost. The choice of any of the methods is dependent on the type and nature of property, the availability of relevant data and the purpose.

#### 1. The Direct Comparison or Market Comparison Method

This method of valuation involves the systematic comparison of value-determining features among similar properties such as lot size, location, financing terms, market conditions at the time of sale, etc and usually the features of the subject property which is being evaluated are compared with several neighboring or similar properties that have been sold recently (known as Comparable properties).

The sale price of each Comparable is adjusted to reflect the ways in which the subject property is more or less different from the Comparable. There would not have been adjustments if the Comparable properties were exactly like the subject property and the transactional details were the same. However, the real estate market is imperfect and no two properties are exactly alike and therefore some forms of adjustment is almost always required.

After the adjustment, the valuer reconciles the final adjusted prices into an indicated value.

In adopting the sale comparison approach a careful value analysis was made on recently completed sale transactions of similar properties as well as the quoted prices of residential properties currently on sale in the same or similar neighborhoods.

Appropriate adjustments were also made in the sale prices to take account of the differences between the subject property and the Comparable in order to arrive at a reasonable value for the subject property.

#### 2. Income Capitalization Approach

This approach involves the capitalization of estimated Net Income from the property. This is arrived at by estimating the Potential Gross Income (PGI), which is the total annual income the property would produce if it were fully developed and rented and had no collection losses.

From the Potential Gross Income, allowance was made for possible rate of vacancy and collection loss to arrive at Effective Gross Income. The operating expenses that would be incurred in operating the property are also estimated and subtracted from the Effective Gross Income.

Appropriate discounting factor is applied to the series of net operating incomes to arrive at the discounted future incomes which we aggregated to arrive at the fair market value for the property.

#### 3. Depreciated Replacement Cost Approach

The Cost Approach estimates the capital value of the property by assessing the gross replacement cost of the buildings and other improvements based on current labour and building material prices and also building construction techniques.

An accrued depreciation based on such factors as age, state of repair, functional and environmental obsolescence is then estimated and subtracted from the gross replacement cost of the subject property to obtain the Net Replacement Cost (depreciated cost).

The value of the land is then obtained by the sale comparison or the market comparison approach and added to arrive at the value of the property.

### CHOICE OF METHOD

In the valuation of the subject property, the Depreciated Replacement Cost Method and Market Comparison Approach were adopted to find the value of the property. The reasons are as follows:

#### The Depreciated Replacement Cost Method

1. The nature of the building is very different from most houses in the neighbourhood. Also, type of finishing, fittings and construction were considered
2. There is not enough evidence of recent sale of properties with similar attributes on the market within the area
3. Considering the inflationary nature of our economy this method takes into account the current cost of replacing an asset while factoring in depreciation. This provides a more accurate reflection of an asset's value

#### The Market Comparison Method

1. This method was used to gather information on the prevailing land values in the area and making room for adjustments, taking into consideration the subject properties and their access to road network, electricity, water, etc.

The result from this approach is therefore deemed fair and gives a true reflection of the market value of the subject property.
