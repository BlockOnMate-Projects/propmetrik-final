# PROPMETRIK Insights & Press Architecture

This document defines the Information Architecture (IA), Navigation (Menu/Footer), and Publication Standards for the PROPMETRIK Insights and Press platform. Benchmarked against industry giants (Fitch, Moody's, S&P Global, JLL), this architecture ensures PROPMETRIK's position as the primary authority in Ghana's real estate data.

---

## 1. Global Navigation Architecture

The platform uses a "Multi-Channel" navigation strategy to serve different user personas (e.g., Journalists looking for quotes vs. Institutional Investors looking for raw data).

### 1.1 Main Menu (Top Navigation)

| Menu Item | Sub-Categories (Dropdown) | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Insights & Research** | • **Market Intelligence**: Economic Drivers, Inflation/FX Impact, PPI.<br>• **Commercial Property Monitor**: Demand Index, Coworking Trends, Service Charge OCM.<br>• **Housing Affordability (GHAI)**: Regional affordability & Mortgage Accessibility scores.<br>• **Ghana-Specific Research**: Diaspora Investment, Land Title Security, Infra Impact. | Standard-setting analytical content (Reports & Articles). |
| **Terminal (Data)** | • **Live Tickers**: Real-time National & Regional Construction Cost (CCI).<br>• **Sentiment Index**: AI "Market Mood" from news & social media.<br>• **Interactive Heatmaps**: Price, Demand, and Investment Hotspots.<br>• **Investment Analytics**: Yield Benchmarks, Portfolio Peer-Comparison, ROI Models. | Data-rich dashboards and interactive modeling tools. |
| **Press & Media** | • **Press Releases**: Official PropMetrik news.<br>• **In the Media**: External coverage, Interviews, and Op-Eds.<br>• **Media Kit**: Standard Brand Assets (Logos, Valuer Bios).<br>• **Data for Journalists**: Attribution-ready charts & "Instant Quotes". | Resource portal for editors and journalists. |
| **Resources** | • **Methodology**: Transparency on GMTI and RICS compliance.<br>• **Events & Webinars**: Regional Outlook sessions.<br>• **Expert Network**: Professional Advisory & Expert Commentary. | Proof-of-quality and advisory support. |

### 1.2 Global Footer (Authority-First)

*   **Market Analysis**: Sector Reports, Regional Tiers, Construction Costs, Affordability Index (GHAI).

## 2. Analytical Document Standards

Benchmark: **Fitch RatingsNavigator** & **S&P Research Reports**.

### 2.1 The "Authority" Report Structure (PDF & Web)
1.  **Investment Thesis / Executive Summary**: Top 1-page takeaway.
2.  **Macroeconomic Flash**: Ghana-specific drivers (GHS/USD, Inflation).
3.  **Data Core**: Charts, Tables, and Heatmaps (Powered by `Analytics.md`).
4.  **Risk Sensitivity Score (Fitch Style)**: A matrix showing how "Best Case" and "Worst Case" scenarios affect value.
5.  **AI Insight Sidekick**: A 1-paragraph synthesis generated for each major section.
6.  **Methodology & Sources**: Essential for institutional credibility.

### 2.2 Dynamic Publication Flow
*   **Live Update**: The "Terminal" updates daily/weekly based on scrapers.
*   **Monthly Digest**: A synthesized PDF generated on the 1st of every month.
*   **Quarterly Outlook**: A deep-dive 20+ page document with long-term forecasts.

## 3. Press Room & Media Integration

### 3.1 Media Relations Module
*   **Public Access**: Journalists can download "Lite" charts and data visualizations for their articles (with PropMetrik attribution).
*   **Quote Generator**: AI-driven "Instant Quotes" based on current market sentiment data for journalists on tight deadlines.
*   **Branded Receipt Generation**: Every press release or media kit download includes a professional summary.

## 4. UI/UX Features for Marketing Pages

### 4.1 "Lite" vs. "Professional" Tiers
*   **Lite (Marketing Page)**: Static heatmaps, historical data tickers (delayed), and "Executive Summary" views.
*   **Professional (Terminal)**: Street-level drill-downs, live data tickers, custom report building, and ML predictive modeling.

### 4.2 Lead Funnels
*   **"Download Methodology"**: Leads for institutions.
*   **"View full Sentiment Index"**: Leads for investors.
*   **"Book an Expert"**: Leads for advisory services & media coverage.
