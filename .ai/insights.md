## 1. Strategic Vision & Positioning

### 1.1 The Gap We Fill

Ghana and West Africa lack a credible, data-driven real estate intelligence platform comparable to what Moody's, CBRE, JLL, and Knight Frank provide for mature markets. Government agencies (Bank of Ghana, Ghana Statistical Service, Lands Commission) publish fragmented data. International firms cover Africa at continent level with minimal Ghana granularity. PROPMETRIK fills this void.

### 1.2 Positioning Statement

> **PROPMETRIK Research** is the institutional-grade source for Ghana real estate data, analytics, and market intelligence — delivering the indices, forecasts, and policy-relevant research that economists, banks, fund managers, and government bodies rely on.

### 1.3 The Problem with Many Titles

The original architecture listed 15+ separate publication titles across 6 cadences — creating production overhead, audience confusion, and a fragmented website experience. Readers didn't know which report to read. The production pipeline was spread across too many templates. The website navigation was sprawling.

**The solution**: collapse everything into **two clearly defined products** that together cover all analytics, all content, and all cadences.

### 1.4 Competitive Benchmarking

| Benchmark Firm | What We Adopt | PROPMETRIK Equivalent |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Moody's** | Ratings methodology, risk scoring, economic data products | Ghana Property Risk Scores, Economic Impact Reports |
| **Fitch Ratings** | Structured report sections, sector analyst model | Outlook report structure, named analysts |
| **S&P Global** | Market intelligence platform, data subscriptions, research tiers | Terminal data feed, tiered API access, research library |
| **JLL** | Global Real Estate Perspective (monthly), tech/AI focus, regional outlooks | Ghana Real Estate Outlook (monthly), PropTech commentary |
| **Knight Frank** | Property sector taxonomy, topic-based navigation, flagship publications (Wealth Report, Africa Horizons) | Sector-based research, Annual Outlook with Horizons appendix |
| **CBRE** | MarketBeat quarterly reports, weekly podcast, content pillars | Ghana Real Estate Outlook (quarterly), PROPMETRIK Perspectives podcast |
| **Cushman & Wakefield** | MarketBeat local reports, AI Impact Barometer, topic filters | Regional snapshots within the Outlook, AI-assisted generation |
| **Colliers** | Property Snapshots (monthly), Capital Markets Snapshots (quarterly), Global Investor Outlook (annual) | Ghana Property Snapshot (weekly), Ghana Real Estate Outlook (annual) |

## 2. The Two-Product Model

### 2.1 Overview

All PROPMETRIK research flows into **two branded publication products**. Nothing is orphaned. Nothing is redundant. Every piece of data, every index, every sector analysis feeds one of two places.

| | **Product 1** | **Product 2** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Name** | **Ghana Real Estate Outlook** | **Ghana Property Snapshot** |
| **Subtitle** | The Comprehensive Report | The Weekly Market Brief |
| **Cadence** | Monthly · Quarterly · Annual | Weekly (Every Monday 8AM GMT) |
| **Format** | 8–80 pages depending on edition | 400–600 words + charts |
| **Purpose** | All analytics, indices, sector data, and forecasts — combined into one authoritative publication at each cadence | A lean, scannable weekly brief. One lead story, key data updates, and a curated chart. Keeps the audience engaged between the big reports. |
| **Audience** | Subscribers → Institutional investors → Board-level | All subscribers, journalists, general professionals |

### 2.2 The North Star

> Every piece of data, every index, every sector analysis PROPMETRIK produces feeds one of two places — the **Ghana Real Estate Outlook** or the **Ghana Property Snapshot**. Nothing is orphaned. Nothing is redundant.

## 3. Product 1 — Ghana Real Estate Outlook

### 3.1 Concept

One report name. Three editions distinguished only by depth and cadence. A reader who receives the Monthly Outlook knows exactly what to expect. When they receive the Quarterly, it is the same familiar product — just broader and deeper. The Annual is the definitive edition of the same publication, not a separate title.

### 3.2 Edition Matrix

| Edition | Cadence | Depth | Audience |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Monthly** | 1st of each month | 8–14 pages. All indices refreshed. National snapshot with key sector moves. 3-month outlook. | Subscribers, journalists, general professional |
| **Quarterly** | January · April · July · October | 20–30 pages. Deep sector analysis, regional breakdowns, investment outlook, risk matrix. | Institutional investors, banks, fund managers, government |
| **Annual** | January (full-year edition) | 50–80 pages. Full-year review, 10-year ML forecast, Ghana Horizons regional benchmarking, wealth & luxury appendix. | Board-level, embassies, international institutions, media |

### 3.3 Report Structure — All Three Editions

All three editions share the same section architecture. Monthly editions include all sections in concise form. Quarterly editions expand each section with deeper analysis. The Annual adds two exclusive appendices.

| # | Section | What It Contains |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **01** | **Executive Summary** | 3–5 key findings. Biggest data move of the period. At-a-glance index dashboard (GHPI, CCI, GHAI, GCPI, PSI). Investment thesis in 3 paragraphs. |
| **02** | **Macroeconomic Context** | GDP growth, inflation, GHS/USD, Bank of Ghana policy rate. How macro conditions are moving real estate. Auto-pulled from BoG and GSS APIs. |
| **03** | **Residential Market** | Ghana House Price Index (GHPI) update. Sales and rental price trends by region. Affordability (GHAI). Mortgage market. New supply pipeline. |
| **04** | **Commercial Market** | Office vacancy rates and prime rents. Retail performance. Co-working uptake. Ghana Commercial Property Index (GCPI) update. |
| **05** | **Construction & Development** | Construction Cost Index (CCI) — materials, labour, overhead. Building permits issued. Active development pipeline. Regional project tracker. |
| **06** | **Investment & Capital Markets** | Transaction volumes. Yield benchmarks. FDI flows. Diaspora Investment Index (DII). Capital Markets Review. Cap Rate Tracker. |
| **07** | **Regional Snapshots** | Accra · Kumasi · Takoradi: each city gets a 1-page data panel — prices, rents, vacancy, construction activity, investment highlights, 3-month outlook. |
| **08** | **Risk & Resilience** | Ghana Property Risk Score (GPRS) by region. Title security, flood risk, regulatory outlook. Best/Base/Worst scenario matrix. |
| **09** | **Outlook & Forecasts** | 3-month forward view (Monthly). 12-month with sector projections (Quarterly). ML-powered 10-year price forecast (Annual only). |
| **10** | **Methodology & Disclaimer** | Index methodologies, data sources, confidence intervals, RICS compliance note, legal disclaimer. |

### 3.4 Depth Scaling by Edition

Each section scales in depth based on the edition:

| Section | Monthly (8–14pp) | Quarterly (20–30pp) | Annual (50–80pp) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Executive Summary | 1 page, 3 findings | 1.5 pages, 5 findings | 2 pages, 7 findings, investment thesis |
| Macroeconomic Context | 0.5 page summary | 1.5 pages with charts | 3 pages with YoY comparison tables |
| Residential Market | 1 page headline numbers | 2–3 pages with affordability deep-dive | 5 pages + mortgage market + supply pipeline |
| Commercial Market | 0.5 page headline | 2 pages all sub-sectors | 4 pages + co-working + emerging sectors |
| Construction & Development | 1 page CCI + key moves | 2 pages component breakdown + permits | 4 pages + regional project tracker |
| Investment & Capital Markets | 0.5 page summary | 2 pages + capital flows + yields | 4 pages + FDI + diaspora + cap rates |
| Regional Snapshots | 1 data panel per city | 1. full page per city | 2 pages per city + secondary cities |
| Risk & Resilience | 0.5 page risk scores | 1.5 pages + scenario matrix | 3 pages + regulatory outlook |
| Outlook & Forecasts | 1 page, 3-month view | 2 pages, 12-month + ML bands | 4 pages, 10-year ML forecast |
| Methodology | 0.5 page standard note | 1 page extended | 2-page appendix |

### 3.5 Annual Edition — Two Exclusive Appendices

The Annual Outlook includes two appendices that do not appear in Monthly or Quarterly editions:

#### Appendix A — Ghana Horizons: Pan-African Benchmarking

Ghana's performance benchmarked against Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, and Côte d'Ivoire. Diaspora capital flow analysis. International investor positioning. Designed for development finance institutions and global fund managers.

#### Appendix B — Wealth & Luxury Property Review

Premium residential market analysis. High-net-worth buyer profiles. Luxury price bands by neighbourhood. HNWI investment patterns. PropTech & innovation commentary.

## 4. Product 2 — Ghana Property Snapshot

### 4.1 Concept

The Snapshot is PROPMETRIK's high-frequency, always-on publication. It is short, scannable, and data-forward — designed to be read in under 5 minutes and to keep the audience engaged every week between the major Outlook editions.

**The Snapshot is NOT a mini version of the Outlook.** It is a distinct format: one focused angle per week — a single market story, chart, or data event — written tightly and distributed via email and web. It drives subscribers back to the full Outlook reports.

### 4.2 Snapshot Structure — Fixed Every Week

The Snapshot uses a locked template. Every edition is identical in structure so readers always know where to look. Total length: 400–600 words plus charts.

| Block | What It Contains |
|:---|:---|
| **01 — This Week's Lead** | One market story or data event. Could be a CCI move, a major transaction, a policy announcement, or a trend emerging from the data. Written as a 200-word article with a single chart. |
| **02 — Index Pulse** | A 4-cell data panel showing the week's most-moved indices: GHPI, CCI, PSI (Sentiment), and one rotating index. Each cell shows: current value, weekly change, and a 4-week sparkline. |
| **03 — Chart of the Week** | One standout chart pulled from the analytics engine. Could be a price heatmap, a CCI regional comparison, an affordability trend, or a forecast cone. 2-sentence AI-written caption. |
| **04 — What to Watch** | Three bullet points. Upcoming data releases, policy decisions, or market events that readers should track in the coming week. Written in plain language — no jargon. |
| **05 — From the Archive** | A one-line teaser linking back to the most relevant Outlook report or article. Drives report readership. Rotated editorially. |

### 4.3 Weekly Content Rotation

The Snapshot rotates its lead focus across the week's data cycle so that no two consecutive editions feel repetitive. The following rotation is the default — it can be overridden whenever a significant market event demands coverage.

| Week | Lead Focus | Typical Data Source / Trigger |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Week 1** | Construction & Costs | Fresh CCI update (published 5th of month). Materials, labour, overhead moves. Development pipeline. |
| **Week 2** | Residential Market | GHPI update, rental price trends, affordability data from GHAI. Housing supply pipeline. |
| **Week 3** | Investment & Capital | Transaction volumes, yield moves, cap rate tracker, diaspora flows, FDI signals. |
| **Week 4** | Economy & Outlook | Macro indicators: BoG rate, GHS/USD, inflation. Sentiment Index (PSI). Forward-looking commentary. |
| **Ad-hoc** | Breaking Market Event | Major transaction, policy announcement, index spike, or data anomaly triggers an immediate Snapshot. |

## 5. Standalone Publications

Three content types do **not** fold into the Outlook or the Snapshot. They have distinct audiences, irregular cadences, or require human initiation, so they sit outside the automated publication cycle.

| Publication | Cadence | Why It Stays Separate |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Policy Papers** | Ad-hoc | Commissioned or event-driven. Formal PDF format for government and Bank of Ghana. Cannot be auto-generated from market data alone — requires analyst authorship and institutional framing. |
| **Press Releases** | Ad-hoc | Company and product announcements. Require human initiation and editorial approval. Live in the Press Room, not the Insights section. |
| **Podcast — PROPMETRIK Perspectives** | Weekly (Thursday) | Audio format with a distinct production workflow. Covers similar themes to the Snapshot but is a conversation, not a data report. Cross-references the weekly Snapshot for listener continuity. |

#### 11.2.1 Autopilot Mode — Outlook & Snapshot

The Outlook and Snapshot run on fully autonomous pipelines at their defined cadences. No human clicks a button — the system generates, validates, and publishes on its own.

```
CRON TRIGGER (at scheduled time)
│
├── STEP 1: DATA COLLECTION
│   ├── Identifies edition type (Monthly/Quarterly/Annual Outlook, or Snapshot)
│   ├── Fetches latest data from all relevant Analytics API endpoints
│   ├── Pulls macro context (BoG rates, GSS inflation, GHS/USD) from Data Hub
│   ├── Retrieves ML forecasts and confidence intervals from ML pipeline
│   ├── Compares current values against last edition to detect changes
│   └── Assembles data context payload for AI generation
│
├── STEP 2: AI GENERATION
│   ├── For Outlook: generates all 10 sections at depth matching the edition
│   ├── For Snapshot: generates all 5 blocks using the weekly rotation lead
│   ├── Selects charts from analytics catalog (count varies by edition)
│   ├── Generates AI insight per chart
│   ├── Generates SEO metadata and social card copy
│   └── Output: complete publication object ready for validation
│
├── STEP 3: QUALITY GATES (see Section 18.4)
│   ├── Structural, factual, quality, freshness, and deduplication checks
│   ├── ALL PASS + confidence ≥ 0.75 → auto-approve
│   └── ANY FAIL → defer to human review queue
│
├── STEP 4: AUTO-APPROVE
│   ├── Status: "ai_draft" → "approved"
│   ├── Author: "PROPMETRIK Research"
│   ├── Audit trail logged
│   └── PDF auto-generated (Outlook editions)
│
└── STEP 5: AUTO-PUBLISH (Multi-Channel)
    ├── Web: published to public site
    ├── PDF: uploaded to MinIO (Outlook)
    ├── Newsletter: queued for distribution
    ├── Social: cards posted
    ├── API: notification to Enterprise subscribers
    └── Status: "approved" → "published"
```

#### 11.2.2 Manual Mode — Press Releases & Policy Papers

Press Releases and Policy Papers require human initiation. They cover corporate announcements and commissioned analysis that cannot be auto-generated from market data.

```
STEP 1: Admin enters topic + key points + tone
STEP 2: AI generates full draft
STEP 3: Human reviews and edits in rich editor
STEP 4: Approval (+ optional embargo date) → Publish
```

## 18. Autonomous Publication Pipeline

This section specifies the cron-driven, fully autonomous system that generates, validates, approves, and publishes the **Ghana Real Estate Outlook** and **Ghana Property Snapshot**. The goal is zero-touch operation.

### 18.1 Design Principles

1. **Every publication reads like a senior analyst wrote it.** The AI writes flowing prose with embedded data, contextual analysis, and forward-looking commentary — the same way a human analyst at CBRE or JLL would write.
2. **Charts are not decoration — they are evidence.** Every chart is selected because the data tells a story. Chart insights reference the visual ("As Figure 3 illustrates...").
3. **Quality gates replace human approval.** Structured validation (factual accuracy, tone, freshness, deduplication) ensures every auto-published article meets institutional standards.
4. **Failures degrade gracefully.** If data is stale, confidence is low, or a quality gate fails — the publication is deferred to the human review queue, never published with bad data.
5. **Press Releases and Policy Papers are always manual.** Corporate communications and commissioned analysis require human judgment.

### 18.2 Cron Schedule — Two Products

| Publication | Cron Expression | Data Sources | Charts | Template |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Snapshot — Weekly** | `0 8 * * 1` (Mon 8AM GMT) | Rotating focus endpoints (see § 4.3) + PSI + top movers | 2 (lead + chart of week) | Snapshot 5-block template |
| **Snapshot — Ad-hoc Breaking** | Event-driven (anomaly detector) | Anomaly trigger endpoint | 1 (trigger metric) | Snapshot template (lead block overridden) |
| **Outlook — Monthly** | `0 8 1 * *` (1st of month, 8AM) | Full analytics suite + macro data | 6–10 | 10-section Outlook (concise) |
| **Outlook — Quarterly** | `0 8 1 1,4,7,10 *` (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct 1st) | Full analytics + ML forecasts + capital flows | 10–15 | 10-section Outlook (deep) |
| **Outlook — Annual** | `0 8 15 1 *` (January 15th) | Full year analytics + 10-year ML + cross-border + luxury | 20–30 | 10-section Outlook (definitive) + 2 appendices |

**Note**: In months where a Quarterly or Annual edition publishes, the Monthly edition is suppressed — the higher-cadence edition subsumes it.

### 18.3 AI Generation — Writing Standards

#### 18.3.1 Style Rules

```
1. LEAD WITH INSIGHT, NOT DATA
   ✗ "The CCI rose 3.2% month-over-month in January 2026."
   ✓ "Construction costs in Ghana posted their sharpest monthly increase
      since Q3 2025, with the CCI climbing 3.2% in January — driven
      primarily by a 7.8% spike in cement prices following the new
      import levy announced in December."

2. EXPLAIN CAUSATION
   ✗ "Office vacancy in Accra fell to 14.2%."
   ✓ "Accra's office market tightened further in Q4, with vacancy
      compressing to 14.2% from 16.1% the prior quarter. The decline
      reflects sustained absorption in Airport City and East Legon,
      where three multinational tenants collectively leased 8,200 sqm
      of Grade A space."

3. PROVIDE FORWARD CONTEXT — ALWAYS INCLUDE PROJECTIONS
   ✗ "Residential prices rose 6.2% YoY."
   ✓ "Residential prices rose 6.2% year-over-year, but the pace of
      appreciation is decelerating — down from 8.4% in Q2. With Bank
      of Ghana holding the policy rate at 27%, mortgage costs continue
      to constrain demand. Our ML models project price growth moderating
      to 4–5% over the next two quarters, with a 90% confidence interval
      of 3.1–6.2%."

4. REFERENCE CHARTS NATURALLY
   ✗ "See the chart below for CCI trends."
   ✓ "As Figure 2 illustrates, the divergence between material costs
      (up 4.1%) and labor rates (flat at 0.3%) has widened to its
      largest gap in 18 months."

5. USE ANALYST VOICE
   First-person plural: "we expect", "our data shows", "in our view".
   This is PROPMETRIK Research speaking. Never "I" or passive voice.

6. BALANCE BULLISH AND BEARISH
   Every outlook section MUST note at least one upside and one downside risk.

7. PROJECTIONS ARE MANDATORY
   Monthly Outlook: 3-month forward projections per sector
   Quarterly Outlook: 12-month forward projections with ML confidence bands
   Annual Outlook: 10-year ML-powered price/yield/affordability forecasts
```

#### 18.3.2 Chart Selection Intelligence

```
CHART SELECTION ALGORITHM:

1. IDENTIFY BIGGEST MOVERS
   → Query all relevant endpoints for the period
   → Rank by: absolute change, % change, deviation from trend
   → Top 3 movers become mandatory charts

2. MATCH TO SECTION STRUCTURE
   → Outlook § 01: single most impactful data point
   → Outlook § 02: GDP/inflation/rates chart
   → Outlook § 03–08: most relevant chart per section
   → Outlook § 09: ML forecast chart with confidence bands
   → Snapshot: lead chart matches rotation topic, Chart of the Week = biggest mover

3. VISUAL DIVERSITY
   → Never repeat same chart type consecutively
   → Mix: line (trends), bar (comparisons), heatmap (geographic),
     forecast (ML bands), donut (composition)

4. DATA SIGNIFICANCE THRESHOLD
   → Don't chart flat data (< 0.5% change) unless flatness IS the story

5. PROJECTIONS IN EVERY EDITION
   → Monthly: at least 1 forecast chart (3-month horizon)
   → Quarterly: at least 2 forecast charts with confidence bands
   → Annual: dedicated forecast section with 10-year projections
```

### 18.5 Anomaly-Triggered Snapshot (Breaking Edition)

```typescript
class BreakingSnapshotTrigger {
  // Runs every 30 minutes, checks all analytics endpoints
  @Cron('*/30 * * * *')
  async checkForAnomalies(): Promise<void> {
    const thresholds = {
      priceIndexChange: 2.0,    // GHPI moves > 2% in a day
      cciSpike: 3.0,            // CCI moves > 3% MoM
      vacancyShift: 2.0,        // Office vacancy moves > 2 ppt
      volumeSpike: 50,          // Transaction volume > 50% above 30d average
      sentimentSwing: 15,       // PSI moves > 15 points
    };

    const anomalies = await this.detectAnomalies(thresholds);

    for (const anomaly of anomalies) {
      if (await this.snapshotsPublishedToday() >= 2) break;  // Rate limit
      if (await this.recentlyFlashedMetric(anomaly.metric, 48)) continue;  // Dedup
      await this.autopilotPipeline.executeSnapshot({ breaking: true, ...anomaly });
    }
  }
}
```
